Benchmarks

What do multiplexing, serialization and flow control actually cost, compared to writing the bytes into a socket yourself?

Numbers are still being collected. They will show up here.

Until then you can run the suite yourself. It compares a plain TCP socket, a raw multiplexer port and the different channel types, and reports throughput, messages per second and CPU time per gigabyte. Links with limited bandwidth and added latency can be emulated, so you can see what happens on a slow or distant connection.
